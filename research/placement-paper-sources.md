## Official pattern and assessment references (not papers)

- TCS All India NQT hiring page: https://www.tcs.com/careers/india/tcs-all-india-nqt-hiring
  - Published pattern on the page: Foundation 75 minutes (Numerical, Verbal, Reasoning; 25 minutes each) and Advanced 115 minutes (Advanced Quantitative & Reasoning 25 minutes, Advanced Coding 90 minutes).
  - This is a current hiring-pattern reference, not a previous-year question paper.
- TCS iON NQT sample-test hub: https://www.tcsion.com/hub/national-qualifier-test/
  - Offers sample tests for Cognitive, IT, BFSI and Non-Tech.
- Accenture assessment preparation guidance: https://www.accenture.com/in-en/careers/explore-careers/area-of-interest/journey-to-accenture
  - Describes online technical assessments, coding environment, job-relevant problem solving and HackerRank sample-test preparation. It is guidance, not an Accenture past paper.
- Wipro careers portal: https://careers.wipro.com/
  - No company-hosted campus aptitude/placement question-paper PDF was verified in this research pass.

## Third-party / recalled material (use cautiously)

- TCS candidate-reported and reconstructed paper collections exist on preparation sites. These are not official TCS papers unless a source explicitly links to a company-published document. Example overview: https://placementpapers.app/tcs/previous-year-question-papers/
- Accenture previous-question collections are also third-party/candidate-recalled. They should be labelled REPORTED or RECONSTRUCTED, never OFFICIAL. Example overview: https://placementpapers.app/accenture/previous-year-question-papers/

## Analysis for PrepAI

### Pattern signals supported by the inspected samples
1. **TCS sample:** verbal comprehension, sentence ordering, vocabulary/idioms, grammar and mixed aptitude, plus a programming task. The newer TCS NQT hiring page separately documents Foundation and Advanced sections, including coding.
2. **Infosys sample:** multi-step reasoning (including data interpretation and data sufficiency), deductive/logical reasoning, mathematical reasoning and a technical section. This sample is 100 minutes and should not be mistaken for a current guaranteed test blueprint.
3. **Infosys HackWithInfy:** coding problems are algorithmic and constraint-driven; keep this as a separate coding-practice track, not mixed into the Systems Engineer aptitude paper.
4. **Accenture:** the official candidate guidance emphasizes applied problem solving and technical assessments in a coding environment. Build role-specific coding practice rather than claiming a fixed universal aptitude paper.
5. **Wipro:** no official paper verified here. Until a company-issued source is found, use clearly labelled Wipro-pattern practice, not “official Wipro PYQ”.

### Recommended source labels in the question bank
- OFFICIAL_SAMPLE: published by the company as a sample.
- OFFICIAL_PATTERN: published by the company as hiring/test structure or preparation guidance.
- REPORTED_RECALL: candidate-recalled question, with a citation and date if known.
- RECONSTRUCTED: third-party compilation or reconstructed paper.
- GENERATED_PRACTICE: original PrepAI question written to match a documented topic/style.

### Import and copyright safeguards
- Preserve source URL, source title, classification, and verification date for every imported item.
- Do not bulk-copy entire copyrighted papers into PrepAI without permission. Prefer recording source metadata and creating original, independently written questions based on the documented skills and patterns. Link users to the original PDFs for source material.
- Do not assign a year/shift or claim “previous-year” unless the provenance is independently supported.
